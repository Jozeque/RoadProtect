# Getdismissed — Competitive / Strategic Reference

**Relationship to Road Protect**: Backer / strategic investor. **Not a competitor in Israel.** Adjacent player in the US ticket-dismissal space.

## What they do

Getdismissed handles traffic-ticket dismissals in the US — mostly California originally. The product helps drivers contest tickets, file paperwork, and ultimately get fines dismissed. The model is mass-volume, automated drafting + legal infrastructure.

## Why they matter to Road Protect

1. **Proof point** that the playbook works at scale in a different jurisdiction.
2. **Backers** = aligned interests, possibly source of US-market learnings.
3. **Playbook** — pricing, funnel shape, churn dynamics are all relevant priors when modeling Road Protect's trajectory.
4. **Cross-pollination potential** — UX patterns, marketing angles, retention mechanics from a more mature market.
