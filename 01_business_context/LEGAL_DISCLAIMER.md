## English (working translation)

> Road Protect provides a digital environment and technological tools for the self-management and self-drafting of appeal documents related to traffic fines. **The service does not constitute legal advice, is not provided within the framework of a law firm, and does not include representation before authorities on your behalf.** The system creates an initial draft based on the information and selections you have provided; **the responsibility lies solely with you** to review the draft, edit it, verify factual accuracy, and submit the request in accordance with applicable requirements and rules. Use of the service does not constitute a substitute for professional legal advice or representation.

## What this means for product and copy decisions

**Allowed:**
- "We help you draft your appeal."
- "Our experts work on your draft so you don't pay an unjust fine." (the "experts" framing is fine, *not* "lawyers" or "legal representation")
- "Fight your fine with Road Protect."
- "Submit your appeal in minutes."
- "We monitor for you, alert you, and help you respond."

**Forbidden:**
- "Our lawyers will represent you."
- "We guarantee your fine will be cancelled."
- "We submit on your behalf." (the user submits — even on VIP, where we facilitate, the user is the legal author)
- "Legal advice from Road Protect."
- Any phrasing that implies a lawyer-client relationship.

**Gray area — handle with care:**
- "Lawyer referral" on VIP is fine — it's referral, not representation.
- "Experts" is fine as a general term for the drafting team.
- "Fights for you" / "battles on your behalf" is OK as long as the underlying mechanism (drafting, not representation) is clear elsewhere in the surface.

## When in doubt

Read the current bot scenarios in `06_bot/scenarios/` — they're already calibrated to the legal line. Mirror their phrasing.

If a piece of copy makes a claim that would only be true if Road Protect were a law firm, rewrite it.
