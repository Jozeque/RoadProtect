# Plans & Pricing — Reference Card

*Snapshot of the live pricing page as of 2026-05-26. Update when pricing changes.*

## Detection — Basic
- **Price**: ₪99/year (₪8.25/mo equivalent)
- **Billing**: Annual
- **What's in it**:
  - 24/7 fine radar across all authorities
  - Unlimited active alerts
  - Digital fine upload to the system
  - Payment-deadline reminders
- **Role in the funnel**: Entry / awareness product. The free-trial-equivalent for B2C; also the bundled freebie for Trademobile car buyers (1 year).

## VIP — Full Road Protection ⭐ **Recommended**
- **Price**: ₪489/year (₪40.75/mo equivalent) — site frames this as "31% saving"
- **Billing**: Annual
- **What's in it**:
  - Automated 24/7 monitoring across all authorities (= Detection)
  - **Unlimited appeals for a full year**
  - Late-fee absorption — company absorbs the 50% interest if it accrues during our handling
  - Payments to municipalities (we handle the actual transfer)
  - Lawyer referral on a case-by-case basis
- **Role in the funnel**: The margin product. Where the business actually makes money.

## One-off Appeal
- **Price**: ₪49 one-time
- **Billing**: Single payment
- **What's in it**:
  - Full handling of one appeal vs. the authority
  - WhatsApp updates throughout
  - **No time limit on redemption**
- **Role in the funnel**: For prospects who got a single fine and refuse to commit to a subscription. Conversion lever to VIP later.

## Coupon mechanics

One code: **`SAVE30` (30% off), applied to the one-off appeal (₪49) — not to VIP.** It fires only for users who **started a single appeal and abandoned it before payment** (status = `basic_details` / `violation_details`).

Per Yossi's decision (chat, 28/04–30/04):
- **No 50% code**, and **no "30%-or-50% your choice"** in messages — one coupon per message.
- The discount is on the **current appeal**, never on a subscription.
- Conversion is **gradual**: close the cheap appeal first so the user experiences the system end-to-end, then upsell VIP in a *separate, later* flow.
- **Cold leads and no-fine cohorts get no coupon** — VIP is already low-priced, and discounting it trains the audience to wait for deals.

## Pricing tests / ideas to consider

- **Monthly VIP option** (e.g. ₪59/mo, no annual lock) to reduce top-of-funnel friction at the cost of LTV.
- **Family / multi-vehicle plan** — second vehicle at 50%? Real-world demand signal needed.
- **Fleet plan** for SMB (5–50 vehicles) — currently bespoke per partner; productize.
- **Appeal-success guarantee variant** — "we'll refund the appeal fee if it fails" — would change perception but requires actual success-rate data and legal review.
