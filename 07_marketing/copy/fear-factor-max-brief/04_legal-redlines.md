# Legal Red Lines — What the Copy Must NEVER Say

These aren't style preferences — crossing them creates real legal/regulatory exposure. Non-negotiable.

## About Road Protect

- ❌ **Never imply we're a law firm or that "our lawyers represent you."** ✅ We're a digital tool; "מומחים", "הצוות שלנו", "מנסחים ומגישים את הערעור בשמך" — yes.
- ❌ **Never promise a fine will be cancelled.** No "נבטל לך את הדוח", no "מבטיחים ביטול", no success-rate numbers ("85% מהדוחות מתבטלים") without a documented source. ✅ "ממקסמים את סיכויי הביטול", "נלחמים בשבילך", "המון דוחות מתבטלים בגלל פגמים".
- ❌ **Never invent stats, user counts, or savings.** The "30,000 נהגים מוגנים" figure is **marketing-stated** — fine to use as-is, but don't inflate it or add new invented numbers.

## About MAX (the legal-expenses product)

- ✅ Frame it as **insurance / coverage provided through a licensed insurance or legal partner** — NOT as Road Protect acting as the lawyer.
- ✅ Always carry the status: **the product is in development and not yet for sale.** It's a **waitlist**, not a purchase. No prices, no "buy now," no guaranteed terms.
- ✅ Add a short disclaimer near the form: *"המוצר בפיתוח וטרם זמין לרכישה. כיסוי ההוצאות המשפטיות יינתן באמצעות שותף ביטוח/עו״ד מורשה; התנאים הסופיים ייקבעו בהשקה."*

## About the camera facts

- ✅ **Attribute the data** to its source (משטרה / רשות הבטיחות בדרכים / חדשות) where it adds credibility.
- ⚠️ **The ₪10,000 phone fine is a proposal** — phrase as "מתוכנן / בכוונת משרד התחבורה", never as current law.
- ❌ Don't state camera counts or specific locations as exact certainties beyond the fact bank (file 01). "מאות מצלמות חדשות" is safe; a precise street address is not.

## Tone guardrail

- We scare with **truth**, not with panic. Fear that's real and sourced converts; exaggerated fear erodes trust and invites complaints. If a line feels manipulative or unprovable — cut it.
