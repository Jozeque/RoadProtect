# The Product — "Road Protect MAX" + the Waitlist

## What MAX is (working name — replaceable)

Road Protect's most complete protection tier. **Everything in VIP, plus a legal-expenses shield that also covers the lawyer's costs** — so if a fine escalates into a lawsuit, a court hearing, or a license-suspension proceeding, the driver isn't alone and doesn't pay out of pocket.

### What's included
- **Everything in VIP:** 24/7 fine radar across all authorities, unlimited WhatsApp + email alerts, unlimited appeals drafted & filed by our experts, late-fee (50% interest) absorption, payments handled to the municipalities.
- **Legal-expenses insurance:** if a fine turns into a lawsuit / reaches court, the lawyer's costs are covered.
- **Personal lawyer support** for complex cases and license-suspension proceedings.
- **Total peace of mind** — from the small fine all the way to a legal proceeding, the driver has a back.

### How it connects to the camera story
The cameras are the *fear*; MAX is the *relief*. The narrative arc the copy should ride:
**More cameras + remote enforcement → more fines → more points → and in the worst cases, lawsuits / suspension. "And if it reaches court — who fights for you, and who pays for the lawyer?" → MAX.**

## Why a waitlist (not a sale yet)

- The product is **still in development** — the waitlist validates demand cheaply before we build.
- It **reduces legal exposure** — we're not selling an insurance product before its terms are finalized.
- It creates **exclusivity** — early registrants get special launch terms.

So the copy's job is **interest + signup**, not a hard sell. Tone: "be first in line," not "buy now."
