## The reform (the backdrop)

- ✅ **The traffic reform took effect on 8 February 2026.** "Light" violations (fine ≤ ₪500 / up to 6 points) moved from a **criminal court process** to a **digital administrative tribunal**. *(Calcalist, Maariv, Israel Hayom)*
- ✅ **Phase 2 (from August 2026):** the tribunal's scope expands to violations of **₪750–1,500**. *(Calcalist)*
- ✅ **Appeals are now digital and online**, no lawyer required, and the police evidence is shown to the driver at the start of the process. → *Implication: the quality of the written appeal matters more than ever.* *(Calcalist, Mako)*

## The cameras

- ✅ **New "average-speed" cameras** measure your speed across a whole road segment (two cameras, start + end), not at a single point. **Slowing down right before the camera no longer helps.** *(ynet, Calcalist, Globes)*
- 🟡 **Scale:** ~**500 new cameras across ~250 segments** (some reports say ~250 cameras across ~125 segments — the plan is being rolled out in stages). **First 120 cameras live in Q2 2026.** *(ynet, Calcalist, TheMarker, Bizportal)* → If you need one clean number, say "**מאות מצלמות חדשות**" or "כ-500 מצלמות".
- ✅ **Mounted on light/utility poles, and ticket you without stopping you.** *(N12 / Mako)*
- ✅ **Active enforcement expected to begin in Q3 2026** — i.e. imminent. *(Calcalist, TheMarker)*
- 🟡 **Deployment focuses on long inter-city routes** where drivers speed for sustained stretches. *(ynet, Calcalist)*
- ✅ **The old orange "A3" boxes are being replaced** by the new smart cameras. *(TheMarker, Bizportal)*

## Remote / digital enforcement

- ✅ **~80% of traffic tickets worldwide are issued digitally — with no officer present** (per Israel's Road Safety Authority); Israel is moving in that direction. *(ynet)*
- ✅ **Police have real-time access to 6,600+ cameras** (6,641 connected by end of 2024, with a plan to add ~5,000 more), including **license-plate recognition (LPR)**. *(law.co.il)*
- 🟡 **Police unveiled an AI-based system for detecting traffic violations.** *(Globes)*
- ✅ **234 new traffic patrol cars added (≈3× the previous fleet); 99 assigned to inter-city roads.** *(ynet, auto.co.il)*

## Penalties

- ⚠️ **Proposed:** fine for using a phone while driving raised to **up to ₪10,000** (Transportation Minister's proposal; not yet final). Phrase as "בכוונת משרד התחבורה / מתוכנן." *(Times of Israel, ynet)*
- ✅ **7 "life-threatening" violations in focus:** phone use, crossing a solid dividing line, running a red light, speeding, drunk driving, driving without a license, driving while suspended. *(Times of Israel, ynet)*

## Consequences of a missed/unpaid fine (Road Protect's own framing — safe to use)

- ✅ **+50% late-payment interest** once a fine passes its due date.
- ✅ **Double fine** accumulates with repeat fines over time.
- ✅ **License points → courses → suspension**; ultimately bank-account liens for unpaid debt.
