# Reference Campaigns — What to Learn From

Real campaigns close to what we're building (camera fear → fine-fighting → legal-expenses product → waitlist). For each: what it is, the link, and the specific lesson for *our* block.

---

## A. Direct analog — fine-fighting + protection membership

### Getdismissed (California) — *our own backer*
- **What:** fights traffic tickets via a 3-step flow (photo the ticket → system builds a court-ready defense → print/sign/mail). Sells **two things: a one-off dismissal AND an annual "Ticket Protection" membership** that covers service fees for future tickets.
- **Steal:**
  1. **Dead-simple 3-step "how it works"** — reduces the fear of "this is complicated."
  2. **Outcome-led proof, not promises:** "if dismissed → no point on your record + bail refunded." Concrete, conditional ("if"), never guaranteed.
  3. **Their membership = our VIP/MAX logic** (pay once, protected for everything future). Same ladder we're building.
- Links: https://getdismissed.com/ · https://getdismissed.com/our-process · https://getdismissed.com/membership/

## B. Closest to MAX — legal-expenses insurance

### LegalShield (US) — "legal insurance like health insurance"
- **What:** subscription legal-expenses plan — pay monthly, get lawyer access + covered trial defense. Founded after the founder's car accident left him unable to afford legal defense.
- **Steal (this is the MAX playbook):**
  1. **The framing:** *"ביטוח לעורך דין, כמו שיש לך ביטוח רכב ובריאות."* Makes an abstract product instantly graspable. Use it for MAX.
  2. **The origin/empathy hook:** a real "it happened to someone, and the lawyer bill was the real disaster" story converts better than features.
  3. **The urgency mechanic we should copy:** their **"Trial Defense Supplement can't be added *after* you've been sued."** → our waitlist version: *"אי אפשר להוסיף מגן משפטי אחרי שכבר נתבעת — בדיוק בשביל זה נרשמים מראש."* Powerful, honest urgency.
- Link: https://www.legalshield.com/legal-plans-overview

## C. Waitlist launches — the signup mechanics

### Robinhood / Superhuman / Notion — the gold-standard pre-launch waitlists
- **What:** built huge demand *before* launch. Robinhood famously drove ~1M signups pre-launch with a **"skip the line" referral waitlist** (share to move up the queue). Superhuman used scarcity + a curated list.
- **Steal:**
  1. **Referral-to-skip-the-line** — "הביאו חבר ועלו ברשימה." Cheap virality, perfect for a waitlist. Strong candidate for our V2.
  2. **Make the reward for signing up explicit:** early-bird terms, launch discount, "be first." We already promise "תנאים מיוחדים לנרשמים מראש" — keep it.
  3. **Keep the form short.** Best practice: every field cuts conversion; email-only converts best. ⚠️ *Tension with us:* we want phone (for WhatsApp) + plate. Compromise: **name + phone required, email + plate optional** — and consider a countdown/"X already joined" counter for urgency.
- Links: https://www.flowjam.com/blog/waitlist-landing-page-examples-10-high-converting-pre-launch-designs-how-to-build-yours · https://viral-loops.com/blog/how-to-build-a-waitlist/

## D. Cautionary tale — what NOT to do

### DoNotPay — the "robot lawyer" that got fined
- **What:** marketed an AI "robot lawyer" to fight tickets. The **FTC fined it $193,000 (2024) for deceptive "AI lawyer" claims**; bar associations threatened legal action; it never argued in court.
- **Avoid (this validates our legal red lines):**
  1. **Never call it a "robot lawyer" or claim to replace a lawyer.** We're a tool + experts + (for MAX) insurance via a licensed partner.
  2. **Never imply guaranteed outcomes or fake authority/quotes** (they faked a press quote). Every claim we make must be true and sourced.
  3. **Over-promising on AI = regulatory + reputational risk.** Our fear copy must lean on *real enforcement facts*, not on overstated AI claims about ourselves.
- Links: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/02/ftc-finalizes-order-donotpay-prohibits-deceptive-ai-lawyer-claims-imposes-monetary-relief-requires · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DoNotPay

## Bottom line — what to copy into our block

- **Fear half:** sourced, concrete enforcement facts (à la news, not hype) → like Getdismissed's "points + bail" concreteness, not DoNotPay's hype.
- **MAX half:** steal LegalShield's "legal insurance like health insurance" framing + the "can't add it after you're sued" urgency.
- **Waitlist:** explicit early-bird reward + short-as-possible form + (V2) referral-to-skip-the-line.
- **Legal line:** DoNotPay is the cautionary proof — no "robot lawyer," no guarantees, insurance via licensed partner, waitlist not sale.
